# DeepSeek-R1 - Incentivizing Reasoning via RL

## Topic
RL-Incentivized LLM Reasoning

## Background
Large language models (LLMs) have achieved notable progress on reasoning tasks, but existing approaches rely heavily on human-annotated chain-of-thought (CoT) trajectories for supervised fine-tuning (SFT), which limits scalability and constrains model reasoning to human-level thought patterns. Reinforcement learning (RL) offers an alternative by guiding models to autonomously explore reasoning strategies through reward signals, without explicit human demonstrations. The DeepSeek-R1 series, built on DeepSeek-V3-Base and trained with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), investigates the potential of pure RL training to incentivize emergent reasoning capabilities in LLMs.

## Limitations & Research Problem
- **Limitation:** Conventional post-training paradigms depend on large volumes of human-annotated reasoning traces for SFT, which are costly, difficult to scale, and often omit critical reasoning components such as self-reflection and verification steps. Model performance is inherently capped by the quality of human annotators. Neural reward models are prone to reward hacking during large-scale RL training.
- **Problem:** Can pure RL training with rule-based rewards—bypassing SFT entirely—incentivize LLMs to autonomously develop advanced reasoning behaviors (e.g., self-verification, reflection, dynamic strategy adaptation) and outperform conventional SFT-based methods on verifiable tasks such as mathematics and coding?

## Contributions
- Introduced DeepSeek-R1-Zero: first demonstration that pure RL (without any SFT) can elicit emergent advanced reasoning behaviors including self-verification, reflection, and "aha moment" phenomena; AIME 2024 pass@1 improved from 15.6% to 71.0%, with cons@64 reaching 86.7%
- Proposed DeepSeek-R1: a multi-stage training pipeline (cold-start SFT, RL with rule-based and language consistency rewards, rejection sampling SFT, second RL with preference rewards) that retains strong reasoning while improving readability, language consistency, and general-task performance
- Adopted GRPO over PPO for large-scale RL, eliminating the value model to reduce computational overhead and using group-level advantage estimation; experiments show GRPO matches or exceeds carefully tuned PPO
- Distilled DeepSeek-R1's reasoning capability into smaller models (1.5B–70B), which significantly outperform instruction-tuned baselines of comparable size on reasoning benchmarks
- Open-sourced all models (DeepSeek-R1 and six distilled variants) to facilitate community research on long CoT reasoning mechanisms

## Methodology
- **DeepSeek-R1-Zero (pure RL):** Trained directly on DeepSeek-V3-Base with GRPO, without any SFT stage. The reward consists of an accuracy reward (rule-based verification of final answer correctness) and a format reward (requiring `<think>` and `<answer>` tags). During training, the model autonomously develops increasing thinking time, self-reflection, and alternative strategy exploration as emergent behaviors
- **GRPO algorithm:** For each question, a group of outputs {o₁, ..., o_G} is sampled; advantage A_i is computed using the group's reward mean and standard deviation; the policy is optimized via a clipped surrogate objective with KL divergence regularization, without requiring a separate value model
- **DeepSeek-R1 multi-stage pipeline:** (1) Cold-start SFT on thousands of human-aligned long CoT examples; (2) First RL stage using rule-based rewards (reasoning tasks) + language consistency reward + model-based rewards (general tasks) with GRPO; (3) Rejection sampling SFT combining RL-generated reasoning data with non-reasoning SFT data; (4) Second RL stage using rule-based rewards and preference-based rewards to further align with human preferences for helpfulness and harmlessness
- **Reward system:** Reasoning tasks use rule-based rewards (answer matching, code compilation/execution); general tasks use a helpful reward model (pairwise preference) and a safety reward model (point-wise safety scoring); a language consistency reward is computed as the proportion of target-language words in the CoT to mitigate language mixing
- **Knowledge distillation:** Reasoning trajectories generated by DeepSeek-R1 are used to SFT-distill Qwen and Llama series smaller models, transferring long-chain reasoning capabilities to compact architectures
