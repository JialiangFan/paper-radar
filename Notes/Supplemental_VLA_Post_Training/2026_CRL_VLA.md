# CRL-VLA: Continual Vision-Language-Action Learning

## Why This Was Added

Complements LifeLong-RFT with a continual reinforcement learning framing.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful if the proposal discusses stability-plasticity trade-offs for safety correction training.

## Method / Contribution

Uses asymmetric regulation to balance retaining old skills while learning new VLA tasks through reinforcement learning.

## Limitations

Safety constraints are not the primary focus; must be adapted for safety-correction data.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: continual-learning, reinforcement-learning, vla-post-training.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
