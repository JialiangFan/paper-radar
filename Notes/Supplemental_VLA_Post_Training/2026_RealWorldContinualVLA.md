# Can VLA Models Learn from Real-World Data Continually without Forgetting?

## Why This Was Added

Adds the most recent real-world continual VLA learning angle.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful for motivating why real-robot data collection should be structured to avoid catastrophic forgetting.

## Method / Contribution

Studies continual learning of VLA models under realistic real-world conditions and analyzes forgetting/adaptation trade-offs.

## Limitations

Very recent work; conclusions may be preliminary and not safety-specific.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: continual-learning, real-robot, vla-post-training.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
