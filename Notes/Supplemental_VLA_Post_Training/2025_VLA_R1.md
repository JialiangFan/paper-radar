# VLA-R1: Enhancing Reasoning in Vision-Language-Action Models

## Why This Was Added

Adds the reasoning/post-training thread to the VLA safety discussion.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful if the proposal frames safety training as improving grounded reasoning before action execution.

## Method / Contribution

Applies reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards / GRPO-style optimization to improve reasoning and action execution in VLA models.

## Limitations

Reasoning quality is not the same as physical safety; still needs real-robot grounding and execution constraints.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: vla-post-training, reasoning, rlvr.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
