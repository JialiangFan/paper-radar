# SENTINEL: A Multi-Level Formal Framework for Safety Evaluation of LLM-based Embodied Agents

## Why This Was Added

Adds a multi-level formal evaluation perspective for embodied agents.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Highly relevant to the semantic-plan-trajectory safety stack in your slides.

## Method / Contribution

Evaluates physical safety across semantic, plan, and trajectory levels using temporal-logic formalization and verification.

## Limitations

Evaluation framework rather than a training method; integration with VLA action policies is still needed.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: agent-safety, formal-verification, temporal-logic.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
