# Using Large Language Models for Embodied Planning Introduces Systematic Safety Risks

## Why This Was Added

Recent systematic evidence that embodied planning introduces safety risks even without malicious prompting.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful for motivating the safety-agent branch and showing why task success is insufficient.

## Method / Contribution

Introduces DESPITE, a benchmark spanning physical and normative dangers with deterministic validation for embodied planning.

## Limitations

Planning benchmark results need to be connected to robot-state/action safety for VLA deployment.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: agent-safety, embodied-planning, benchmark.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
