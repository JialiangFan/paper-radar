# A Framework for Benchmarking and Aligning Task-Planning Safety in LLM-Based Embodied Agents

## Why This Was Added

Adds SafePlan-Bench and Safe-Align for embodied-agent task-planning safety.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Relevant to the safety-agent route and to benchmark design for unsafe task sequences.

## Method / Contribution

Builds SafePlan-Bench with 2,027 daily tasks across 8 hazard categories and proposes Safe-Align to inject physical-world safety knowledge.

## Limitations

Focuses on task planning, not low-level VLA action shielding.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: agent-safety, benchmark, alignment.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
