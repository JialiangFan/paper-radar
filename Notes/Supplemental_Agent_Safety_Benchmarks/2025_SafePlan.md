# SafePlan: Leveraging Formal Logic and Chain-of-Thought Reasoning for Enhanced Safety in LLM-based Robotic Task Planning

## Why This Was Added

Directly supports the safety-agent monitoring/planning direction.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful for framing an agent-level safety monitor that verifies prompts, plans, preconditions, and postconditions.

## Method / Contribution

Combines formal logic with chain-of-thought reasoners for prompt sanity checking, invariant checking, preconditions, and postconditions.

## Limitations

Planning-level safety does not guarantee continuous robot execution safety.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: agent-safety, task-planning, formal-logic.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
