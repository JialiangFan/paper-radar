# X-DiffVLA: X-Embodied Diffusion Action Heads for Vision-Language-Action Models

## Why This Was Added

Very recent cross-embodied action-head work.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Relevant to action representation choices for safety correction and shield distillation.

## Method / Contribution

Introduces a diffusion-based cross-embodied action head to improve transfer across heterogeneous robot end-effectors.

## Limitations

The paper targets transfer and action modeling, not explicit safety constraints.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: diffusion-policy, cross-embodiment, vla-foundation-model.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
