# VLA-Arena: An Open-Source Framework for Benchmarking Vision-Language-Action Models

## Why This Was Added

Adds an open framework for systematic VLA benchmarking.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful infrastructure reference for building a Safe VLA benchmark or reproducing baselines.

## Method / Contribution

Provides a benchmarking framework to evaluate VLA models across tasks and metrics in a standardized way.

## Limitations

Safety-specific semantic/state-action violations may need custom extensions.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: benchmark, vla-evaluation, open-source.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
