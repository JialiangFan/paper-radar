# X-VLA: Soft-Prompted Transformer as Scalable Cross-Embodiment Vision-Language-Action Model

## Why This Was Added

Important cross-embodiment VLA foundation model.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful if the real-robot project needs to discuss embodiment adaptation and data heterogeneity.

## Method / Contribution

Uses embodiment/data-source-specific soft prompts with a scalable Transformer/flow-matching VLA architecture.

## Limitations

Cross-embodiment adaptation improves capability but does not directly address semantic or physical safety.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: vla-foundation-model, cross-embodiment, soft-prompts.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
