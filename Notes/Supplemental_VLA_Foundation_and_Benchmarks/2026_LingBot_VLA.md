# A Pragmatic VLA Foundation Model

## Why This Was Added

Large real-world VLA foundation model with a GM-100 real-robot benchmark.

## Relevance To Safe VLA

Useful for arguing that real-robot data and benchmark design are now central to VLA progress.

## Method / Contribution

Trains a VLA model on large-scale real-world dual-arm manipulation data across multiple embodiments and evaluates on GM-100.

## Limitations

Reported real-world success remains limited, showing that scaling alone does not solve deployment robustness or safety.

## How To Use In Proposal

- Use this paper to support: vla-foundation-model, real-robot-data, benchmark.
- Connect it to the two proposal directions: inherent VLA safety training and safety-agent/runtime monitoring.
- For the real-robot project, ask whether the method provides training data, safety labels, correction targets, evaluation metrics, or a deployable monitor.
